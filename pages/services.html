How We Work

Our Consulting Engagement Framework

A structured 6-phase methodology designed to move seamlessly from diagnostic analysis to sustained operational execution.

01

Operational Diagnostic

Rapid 2 to 4-week assessment of shop-floor workflows, supply chain dependencies, and cost structures to identify baseline EBITDA improvement opportunities.

02

Transformation Blueprint

Collaborative executive workshop defining target state operating model, prioritised quick-wins, detailed financial ROI business case, and execution milestones.

03

Co-Execution & Turnaround

Embedded Vanguard consultants deployed on-site to lead process re-engineering, vendor renegotiations, team re-skilling, and daily operational governance.

04

Sustainable Governance

Establishing digital KPI dashboards, management control loops, and leadership coaching to ensure all operational gains are locked in permanently.

05

Continuous Optimization

Deploying automated monitoring, real-time analytics, and iterative process enhancements to expand operational efficiency gains across global sites.

06

Capability Transfer

Structured training programs, standard operating procedure (SOP) codification, and executive advisory handover to build permanent internal expertise.
